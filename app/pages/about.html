About Vijayanand Foundation

Our trust is work on Social, Psychological, Educational, Medical, Research and Rehabilitation for all.

Everyone have Well-being of mental, physical and economical domain, Our society is representing to diversity and diversity need to equality of thought, then we can achieve to destination of Life.

Vijayanand Foundation focuses on:

Child development
Education for youth
Support to women for independent life
Care of old age people
Marriage guidance (Vadhu–Var Suchak Kendra)
Child ability and aptitude assessment
Career guidance
Personality development
Courses under PACE Institute
