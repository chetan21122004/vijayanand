Assessments

Psychological Testing & Treatment

Standardised tests used at the centre to understand ability, development, learning, personality, and emotional wellbeing — then plan the right support.

Intelligence Quotient (IQ) Test

Measures reasoning, problem-solving, and cognitive ability to guide education and therapy.

Development Quotient (DQ) Test

Reviews a child’s developmental progress across motor, social, and communication skills.

Personality Test

Helps understand temperament, behaviour patterns, and how a person relates to others.

Learning Disability Test

Identifies specific learning difficulties so teaching and therapy can be adapted.

Emotional Quotient (EQ) Test

Looks at emotional awareness, self-regulation, and social skills.

Anxiety, Stress, Depression Test

Screens emotional distress so counselling and treatment can start from a clear picture.
