Awareness

Understanding autism, together

Autism is a way some people experience the world — in how they communicate, learn, play, and sense their surroundings. Awareness is not a slogan. It is noticing each person, slowing down, and offering support that fits them.

Vijayanand Foundation shares autism awareness through short films, counselling, and therapy. Families often come with questions: Why does my child avoid eye contact? Why is school so hard? Early, kind help — speech, behaviour, occupational therapy, and parent guidance — can make daily life easier.

If you want to watch our films or talk to the team, visit the gallery or contact us.

Therapy

Speech therapy for children

Speech therapy helps children form sounds, grow vocabulary, and use language with others. It is useful when speech is delayed, unclear, or when a child struggles to express needs.

At the therapy centre, sessions are practical and warm: play, pictures, and parent training so practice continues at home. Progress is often slow and steady — that is expected.

Read more on our therapy services page.

Counselling

When to seek counselling

Counselling is a conversation with a trained person when worry, school stress, family conflict, or mood changes feel too heavy to carry alone. You do not need a crisis to ask for help.

We offer school, family, career, pre-marriage, old-age, and mental-health counselling, including online sessions. What is shared stays confidential, except where safety requires otherwise.

Start with online counselling or walk in through our offices.

Assessment

Psychological tests, explained simply

Tests are tools, not labels. An IQ test looks at reasoning. A DQ test looks at a child’s development. Personality, learning-disability, EQ, and anxiety screens help the team see strengths and gaps so therapy and school support can be planned fairly.

Results are explained in plain language. They guide next steps — they do not define a child or an adult.
